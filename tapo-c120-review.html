Is it worth buying?

What it does well: 2K video, indoor/outdoor placement, local microSD option and app alerts. It is a lower-cost way to test a specific monitoring need.

Check before buying: It needs continuous power and 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. A microSD card is separate; cloud history needs a plan. Check local privacy rules and avoid pointing it at neighbours or public areas.

How to set it up and use it

  1. Choose a placement with power and reliable 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.
  2. Install the Tapo app and sign in with a TP-Link ID.
  3. Power on the camera; wait for its pairing light to blink red and green.
  4. Add C120 in the app, connect it to Wi-Fi, then test live view and alerts before mounting.

Most webcams are static — you set them up once and stay locked in frame for the whole call. The Link 2 uses onboard AI and a motorized gimbal to track your face and body, reframing automatically as you move around the room, stand up at a whiteboard, or lean back in your chair.

What It Actually Does

A small motorized head pans and tilts to keep you centered using AI subject tracking, and gesture controls can handle actions such as zoom or tracking without returning to the keyboard. Framing modes are designed for use cases such as solo calls, groups and whiteboards.

Who It's For

Best fit: streamers, teachers and presenters who move during calls or need flexible framing. Desk-only users may get better value from a simpler webcam.

Research Verdict

The Link 2 targets a real workflow problem — staying framed while moving. Its value is strongest for people who actually use tracking and presentation features rather than treating it as a normal fixed webcam.

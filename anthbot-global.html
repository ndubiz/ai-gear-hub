Is a robotic mower right for you?

Think about the lawn rather than the gadget. The right robot mower should match your total mowing area, slopes, narrow passages, trees and obstacles, connectivity and the amount of setup you are willing to do.

Buyer checklist

  • Lawn area and layout
  • Navigation/boundary method
  • Obstacle detection
  • Slope capability
  • Zones and scheduling
  • Local warranty and service availability

Choose by bottleneck

What should you pay for first?

Start with the slowest or most expensive part of your current workflow.

If audio is slow

Start with Firefly or ElevenLabs

Firefly now covers speech and background music in the same creative environment; ElevenLabs remains a stronger fit when multilingual dubbing is the main job.

If video production is slow

Start with Google Vids or Canva

Google Vids suits business video and avatar-led explainers, while Canva is useful when the same campaign needs graphics, short video and brand assets.

If handoffs are slow

Start with automation

Use Zapier-style automation when repetitive publishing, routing or admin work is the real bottleneck.

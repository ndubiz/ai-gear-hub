Wyze Cam v4: what to check before buying

Before watching: “under $30,” instant alerts and “sells out fast” are not verified. “No subscription needed” does not include paid cloud recording and advanced detection. The scene imagery is not verified camera test footage.

Our buying review

Wyze lists 2.5K video, color night vision, motion alerts and two-way talk. Color visibility depends on lighting; notification timing depends on your connection and settings.

The v4 needs plug-in power and 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Local recording requires a separately purchased microSD card. Cloud recording and advanced detection require a paid plan. Outdoor use requires the appropriate outdoor power adapter, sold separately.

Verdict: worth comparing if you have nearby power and want local recording. Check availability, delivery to your country and the total cost before ordering.

Research source: Wyze product specifications and requirements, checked 14 September 2026. This is a specification-based review, not a hands-on test.

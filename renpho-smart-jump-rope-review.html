What the device does

RENPHO says its Bluetooth jump rope syncs jump count and workout time with the Renpho Health app. For this site's tech niche, the question is whether that connected feature makes a real routine easier to manage.

Strengths

A measurable short workout without a full watch or ring.

Limitations

Pairing and sync add friction. RENPHO warns that removing batteries before sync can clear unsynced data, and rough surfaces can damage the rope.

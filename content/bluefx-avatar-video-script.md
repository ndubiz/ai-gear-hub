# BlueFX AI Media Machine — Talking-Avatar Video Script

**Length:** 75–90 seconds  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical  
**Style:** Calm, practical and transparent  
**Presenter:** Friendly AI avatar, waist-up framing  
**Disclosure on screen:** Affiliate link — I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:08)

**Visual:** Avatar on a clean purple-and-blue background. Quick icons for video, voice and ads.

**Avatar:** “Creating AI videos often means paying for several different tools. BlueFX says its AI Media Machine brings many of those jobs into one package—but is it worth a closer look?”

**On-screen text:** One AI video toolkit?

## Scene 2 — What it is (0:08–0:23)

**Visual:** Avatar moves to the left; feature names animate on the right.

**Avatar:** “The public offer includes tools for finding successful ad ideas, analyzing video structure, rebuilding scripts and scenes, creating talking avatars, generating voiceovers and producing short-form videos.”

**On-screen text:** Research • Scripts • Avatars • Voice • Video

## Scene 3 — Practical workflow (0:23–0:42)

**Visual:** Simple three-step animation: Find an idea → Plan new scenes → Generate and edit.

**Avatar:** “A possible workflow looks like this: research an advertising concept, study its hook and shot sequence, create an original script for your own product, generate the scenes, and then edit everything before publishing. The important word is original—do not copy another creator’s ad.”

**On-screen text:** Research → Rebuild ethically → Review

## Scene 4 — Price and caution (0:42–1:02)

**Visual:** Avatar beside a price card and a caution icon.

**Avatar:** “When this review was prepared, the public page displayed lifetime access for two hundred and ninety-seven dollars, or three payments of one hundred dollars. Some generation features use credits, and AI Gear Hub has not completed a hands-on test, so verify the current limits and output quality for yourself.”

**On-screen text:** Check current price, credits and terms

## Scene 5 — Who it may suit (1:02–1:17)

**Visual:** Icons for creator, small business and affiliate marketer.

**Avatar:** “It may suit creators, small businesses and affiliate marketers who make frequent promotional videos. It may not suit teams that need cinema-grade control or independently proven performance.”

**On-screen text:** Best fit: frequent marketing content

## Scene 6 — CTA and disclosure (1:17–1:28)

**Visual:** AI Gear Hub logo and a button-style graphic reading “Read the full overview.”

**Avatar:** “Read the full, balanced overview on AI Gear Hub and check the live BlueFX terms before deciding. The link is an affiliate link, so I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.”

**On-screen text:** Find the right AI tools without the noise.

## Production notes

- Use captions throughout.
- Keep background music low beneath the voice.
- Do not use BlueFX logos, screenshots or sales-page images without permission.
- Use generic, original interface graphics for B-roll.
- Confirm the live price before recording or replace it with “check the current price.”
- Do not claim guaranteed income, guaranteed conversions or guaranteed video quality.

What it does

Plaud Note is a slim recorder for in-person meetings and, when supported, phone-call workflows. The app can turn recorded audio into transcripts, summaries and action items. Accuracy varies with noise, speech and language. Check the current official product specifications before relying on any feature.

Best fit

People who attend many meetings and will review notes against the audio. It is a weaker fit for anyone who cannot record with proper consent, needs completely offline transcription, or does not want an ongoing plan.

Three checks before buying

  1. Consent: get permission where required before recording a conversation.
  2. Plan: compare included minutes with your real monthly recording volume and check additional costs.
  3. Workflow: confirm that your phone and meeting setup are supported; a transcription is a draft until checked against the recording.

How to use it responsibly

Tell participants what you intend to record. Follow Plaud's official pairing instructions, make a short test, review the transcript for mistakes, then turn useful points into an action list. Follow your organisation's data policy for storage and deletion. Do not record private conversations without the required consent.

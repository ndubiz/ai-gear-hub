01 · Screen-free recovery

COLMI R02 smart ring

Best question: Can a ring make sleep and activity trends easier to review than a watch? COLMI lists sleep, heart-rate, SpO₂ and activity records in its QRing app, with core records available without a required monthly subscription.

  • Potential fit: Founders who dislike sleeping with a watch and want a simple morning trend check.
  • Limit: Wellness readings are not a diagnosis. Fit matters; COLMI's product page currently mixes R02 and “Ring 1” wording, so confirm the exact model and size before purchase.
  • Germany check: We have not confirmed a matching Amazon.de R02 listing or German delivery from COLMI's store.

03 · Open-ear work calls

Shokz OpenRun Pro 2

Best question: Can open-ear audio support calls while keeping you aware of the room? Shokz lists a dual-driver design, dual microphones, app EQ, USB-C charging and up to 12 hours of playback.

  • Potential fit: Studio owners moving between spaces who need to hear people nearby.
  • Limit: Open-ear listening leaks some sound and a noisy gym may still make calls hard. IP55 means sweat resistance, not swimming use. We have not independently tested mic quality.

For German studios

Choose by workflow, not hype

For a small studio, the camera and open-ear headset have a clear content and communication role. A ring and watch are personal wellness devices; do not treat their readings as medical monitoring or make claims about client outcomes. The jump rope is a connected training aid, with software setup to consider.

Before buying for a team, check charging, phone compatibility, replacement parts, data handling and whether the product can be bought with a German invoice.

A five-step chooser

Pick the tool by the job, not the hype.

Before comparing brands, write down one task you want to make easier. Then use these checks to narrow the options.

1

Name one task

Choose a specific job: draft emails, summarize your own notes, edit a video, research a topic, generate images, or automate a repetitive step. A clear task makes tools easier to compare.

2

Match the workflow

Check whether the tool works where you already work: your browser, documents, design app, video editor, CRM or phone. Look at export options, integrations and how much setup it needs.

3

Try it with a low-risk example

Use a sample or non-sensitive task first. Compare the result with your current method, check how much editing is needed, and verify important facts yourself.

4

Check privacy and usage rights

Read what happens to prompts and uploaded files, available data controls, retention settings and commercial-use terms. Do not paste confidential client, health, financial or identity information into a tool unless you are authorized and its safeguards meet your needs.

5

Compare the full cost

Check plan limits, renewal price, team seats, credits, add-ons, cancellation and whether a paid tier solves a problem you actually have. Start with one tool and upgrade only when the value is clear.

Before you subscribe, ask:

  • What task will this tool improve?
  • Can I test it on my own workflow first?
  • Can I check its output and correct mistakes?
  • Do its privacy terms, limits and renewal price work for me?

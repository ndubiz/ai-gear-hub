Quick verdict

A pocket light to consider for close-up fill or a coloured background accent. Try changing the position of your existing light first. This is not a room-lighting system, and we have not hands-on tested this model.

What the manufacturer specifies

  • Adjustable RGB colour and brightness.
  • Built-in 2000 mAh battery with USB-C? charging.
  • Magnetic back and cold-shoe mounting options.

Who should skip it?

Skip this small panel if you need broad, soft illumination across a room. Check the mount you need for your phone or camera; do not assume a phone clamp or stand is included. Battery runtime varies with settings, so do not plan around a guaranteed all-day runtime.

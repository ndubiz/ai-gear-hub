Plaud NotePin S vs NotePin: Which AI Recorder Fits Better in 2026?

A research-led look at Plaud's wearable meeting recorders, with the newer NotePin S now treated as the current model to compare first.

Updated: August 23, 2026

Research status: This is not a hands-on test. It is based on Plaud's current product pages and support documentation. Features, prices and plan limits can change, so confirm the current details before buying.
Affiliate disclosure: This page may contain affiliate links in the future. If one is added, AI Gear Hub may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The links below currently point to Plaud's official product information.

The original Plaud NotePin remains a compact wearable recorder for in-person conversations, but the NotePin S is now the more useful starting point for most new buyers because it adds a tactile record button and press-to-highlight control while keeping the same general wearable format.

Plaud's own comparison documentation says both models share the same 51 × 21 × 11 mm dimensions, BLE 5.2 transfer, 20-hour continuous-recording rating and 40-day standby rating. The practical difference is control: NotePin S adds a physical record button and on-device highlighting.

What the Plaud ecosystem does

Recordings are managed through Plaud's app/web ecosystem, where supported AI features can turn conversations into transcripts, summaries and shareable outputs. Plaud currently advertises transcription in 112 languages, speaker labels, custom vocabulary, mind maps and workflow integrations. The Starter plan listed on Plaud's German product page includes 300 transcription minutes per month, with paid plans available for higher limits and additional features.

Reasons to consider NotePin S

  • Physical start/stop control is easier to understand than app-only initiation.
  • Press-to-highlight can mark important moments while a conversation is happening.
  • Wearable form factor supports meetings, interviews, lectures and field notes.
  • Current Plaud ecosystem also spans web and desktop workflows.

Important limitations

  • AI transcription and summaries depend on the Plaud service rather than operating as a completely offline workflow.
  • The free transcription allowance may not cover heavy daily use.
  • NotePin S is designed for in-person recording; Plaud says it is not designed to record phone calls.
  • Recording laws and consent rules vary by country and situation.

Which one should you choose?

AI Gear Hub take

Choose NotePin S if you are buying new and want the most straightforward wearable Plaud experience. The tactile recording button and highlight control are small changes, but they solve an everyday usability problem.

Choose the older NotePin only if you find it meaningfully cheaper and do not care about physical recording controls. The core recording time, storage and general Plaud workflow remain similar.

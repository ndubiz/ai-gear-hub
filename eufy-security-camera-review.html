eufy AI Security Camera Review: Smart Security Without Subscriptions?

How local AI detection changes the home security camera equation.

Affiliate disclosure: This page may contain affiliate links. If you buy through one, AI Gear Hub may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This does not affect our opinions.

Most smart security cameras push your footage to the cloud and charge a monthly fee to actually use the AI features — person detection, package alerts, facial recognition. eufy's pitch is different: many of its cameras run AI detection locally on the device and store footage on local storage, so core features work without an ongoing subscription.

What It Actually Does

The camera's onboard AI distinguishes between people, vehicles, packages, and pets in its field of view, and only sends a notification for the events you actually care about — instead of alerting you every time a leaf blows past. Footage is stored locally (on a base station or built-in storage, depending on model), with cloud storage offered as an optional paid add-on rather than a requirement.

What's good

  • Core AI detection and recording work without a subscription
  • Smart alerts cut down on notification fatigue from irrelevant motion
  • Local storage means no recurring cost just to keep footage
  • Battery and solar-powered models simplify outdoor installs

Where it falls short

  • Local storage means footage isn't automatically off-site if the camera or base is stolen/damaged
  • Some advanced AI features (like facial recognition on certain models) still require a subscription
  • Night vision and detection accuracy vary by model and lighting conditions
  • App and base-station setup takes more steps than plug-and-play cloud cameras

Who It's For

Best fit: anyone who wants real security camera functionality without paying a monthly fee indefinitely, and who's comfortable managing local storage. If you specifically want cloud backup as your primary storage or need advanced AI recognition features across the board, budget for the subscription tier on whichever model you pick.

Our Verdict

eufy's local-AI approach solves the most annoying part of smart cameras — the subscription tax — without giving up meaningful detection accuracy. It's a strong pick for anyone who wants security features to just work, on their terms.

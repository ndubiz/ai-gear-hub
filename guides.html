01 · Beginners

Best AI tools for beginners

Start with one clear task: writing, research, video, coding or automation. Prefer tools with transparent pricing, an easy learning curve and a useful free trial or demo. Avoid buying several overlapping subscriptions before you know what workflow you actually need.

02 · Writing

How to choose an AI writing tool

Compare output quality, editing controls, supported formats, privacy terms, usage limits and total monthly cost. Test the tool on your own real-world prompts before committing.

03 · Creator setup

AI + hardware

Creators usually benefit more from solving the weakest part of their workflow than buying everything at once. Prioritize clear audio, reliable video and software that reduces repetitive editing or distribution work.

Browse creator gear →
04 · Value

Free vs paid AI tools

Free plans are useful for testing fit. Paid plans can make sense when higher limits, commercial rights, collaboration or automation save enough time to justify the recurring cost. Always check cancellation and renewal terms.

Compare by business job →
05 · Coding

Choosing an AI coding assistant

Look for language and editor compatibility, code-context limits, privacy controls and clear pricing. Treat generated code as a draft: review, test and secure it before using it in production.
